ABOUT OUR VENUE — ◆ —

An Unexpected Sanctuary in Hyderabad’s Financial Corridor

Beyond the glass and concrete of Hyderabad’s financial corridor lies an unexpected sanctuary. A place where the relentless pace of the city quietly dissolves into open skies, natural harmony, and architectural grandeur. Welcome to Aura Convention and Lawns — an urban oasis conceived to redefine the art of gathering.

Carved into a natural landscape framed by majestic, timeless hillocks, Aura Convention and Lawns merges raw natural topography with refined modern design. Winding botanical promenades, sculpted green terraces, and curated art installations bring texture and soul to every open space. Every angle has been engineered with photogenic intention: from dramatic golden-hour sightlines against the rocky horizon to intimate, glowing garden alcoves. Here, every photograph captures an heirloom memory, and every view invites stillness.

Halls & Spaces

Exquisite indoor ballrooms and vast open-air lawns designed for every celebration scale.

EXECUTIVE PRECISION

Convention Hall

Inside our convention hall, executive ambition meets uncompromising precision. Aura Convention and Lawns is built from the ground up for premier MICE engagements—from high-profile corporate conventions, international tech summits, and product unveils to strategic board retreats and expansive trade exhibitions. With intelligent acoustic engineering, advanced presentation infrastructure, and versatile modular floor plans, we deliver the structured focus, privacy, and flawless execution that global enterprise commands.

UNDER THE NIGHT SKY

Lawns

Under the open night sky, Aura Convention and Lawns offers a versatile canvas designed not only for royal weddings, vibrant sangeets, and grand receptions, but also for large-scale corporate exhibitions, curated art galleries, cultural festivals, and high-impact tech events. Spanning 20,000 sq.ft framed by scenic natural rock formations, the sprawling lawns adapt effortlessly to custom exhibition pavilions, stage setups, tech product zones, and lavish open-air banquets under the stars.

OPULENT CLIMATE COMFORT

Banquet Hall

For occasions that demand flawless climate-controlled comfort without sacrificing an ounce of grandeur, Aura Convention and Lawns proudly presents our pristine Indoor AC Banquet Hall. This opulent, fully air-conditioned space offers an elegant sanctuary for sophisticated celebrations, vibrant pre-wedding sangeets, and exclusive private galas. With plush interiors, customizable ambient lighting, and impeccable acoustics, the banquet hall ensures your guests remain immersed in absolute comfort and luxury, completely insulated from the seasons outside.
